Every episode counted

The episodes the stats are worked out from.

Every episode in the free public No Such Thing As A Fish podcast feed is included, once it has been transcribed. Club Fish extras that aren't in the public feed are left out, and so are the XL versions: the stats use the regular episode everyone can hear. New episodes are added a few days after they come out.

Fish Stats is an unofficial fan project. It isn't made or endorsed by No Such Thing As A Fish or QI. The numbers are worked out from automatic transcripts of the free public episodes. Listen to the show.
