How long one person talks before anyone else gets a word in. A line counts until the next one starts, but never longer than its words take to say at a slow pace, so silences and music don't count. Left out: the first 3 minutes (welcome and theme), the last minute, and solo intros and outros, and the audiobook episodes. Readings still count (Rhys reading out a sighting report). A quick "yeah" from someone else can end up inside a run if the labels miss it.
