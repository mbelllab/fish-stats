Counted from hand-made lists of about 30 cryptids and 170 famous people, with the spellings the transcripts use ("Sasquatch" counts as Bigfoot, "Nessie" as the Loch Ness Monster). A surname on its own counts only when it can't mean anyone else ("Einstein" yes, "Lincoln" no). "Per hour" is per hour of audio, or for a person, per hour of their own talking.

Fish Stats is an unofficial fan project. It isn't made or endorsed by No Such Thing As A Fish or QI. The numbers are worked out from automatic transcripts of the free public episodes. Listen to the show.
