"Per hour" divides by the hours of audio in that year, month or episode, so busy years don't win by default. A search also finds other forms of a word ("ghost" finds "ghosts"). Hosts are named reliably, guests less so.

Fish Stats is an unofficial fan project. It isn't made or endorsed by No Such Thing As A Fish or QI. The numbers are worked out from automatic transcripts of the free public episodes. Listen to the show.
