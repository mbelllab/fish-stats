How they talk

Each host's trademark words, the words that sum up each year, and who has the widest vocabulary.

Show Host

Signature words

Words each host says far more than anyone else on the show. The number is how many times as often they say it.

"As often" is per word spoken, so talking more doesn't help. A word also needs plenty of uses to rank high, so a word said 30 times can't beat one said 3,000 times by luck. Grammar words and names are left out, and so are words used fewer than 30 times or in fewer than 10 episodes. The whole page also skips the business of running the show: the first and last 3 minutes of every episode (intros, sign-offs, plugs), the bonus episodes (Drop Us A Line, Club Fish, compilations), and words like "microphones", "episode", "Club Fish" and "tier". Tap a word to hear them say it.

Word of the year

Words said more that year than in the show's other years, scored the same way. An episode counts a word at most three times, so one episode about potatoes can't win a year on its own. Adverts and the weekly intro are left out. Tap a word to find it in that year.

Vocabulary

How many different words someone uses in every 1,000 words they say, averaged over all their talking. Measuring equal stretches means talking more doesn't win. Only people with at least 10,000 words are shown, with hosts in their colours.

All of this depends on who the transcripts say is speaking. Hosts are named reliably, guests less so.

Fish Stats is an unofficial fan project. It isn't made or endorsed by No Such Thing As A Fish or QI. The numbers are worked out from automatic transcripts of the free public episodes. Listen to the show.
